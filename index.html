Questions

step inside.

What is Vanta? +
Vanta is a personal operating system. It combines tasks, notes, goals, habits, projects, calendar and analytics into one connected environment designed to help you manage direction, not just productivity.
Is Vanta just another task manager? +
No. Tasks are only one layer. Vanta connects what you need to do with why you're doing it, what project it belongs to, which goal it supports and how you're progressing over time.
Why connect everything? +
Because your life isn't divided into isolated applications. A thought can become a project. A project can become a goal. A habit can influence the goal. Vanta makes those relationships visible.
Who is Vanta for? +
Anyone building toward something. Students, founders, creators, researchers, professionals or simply people who want more clarity about where their time and energy are going.
